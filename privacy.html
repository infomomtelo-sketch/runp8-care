Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 10, 2026 · EC Rental Property Management LLC

Title22 ("we," "us") is a facility documentation and compliance tool for California residential care facilities, operated by EC Rental Property Management LLC, based in Fresno, California. This policy explains data collection, usage, and user choices under title22.app.

1. Information we collect

Includes account details, facility and compliance records entered by users, payment processing data handled securely via Stripe, and basic technical/usage logs.

2. How we use information

To provide and secure the Service, power AI features via Anthropic (without training models on your data), manage subscriptions, and provide support.

3. What we do not do

We do not sell personal or facility data, share resident records with advertisers, or use facility records for marketing purposes.

4. Resident and staff information

Facilities control and are responsible for their entered data, complying with relevant regulations like California's CMIA. We act strictly as a service provider storing and processing data on their behalf.

5–11. Storage, Security, Rights & Contact

Data is protected via encrypted cloud infrastructure (Supabase/PostgreSQL) with role-based access. Includes provisions for service providers, data retention, California privacy rights (CCPA/CPRA), children's privacy, updates, and contact info at support@title22.app (EC Rental Property Management LLC, Fresno, California).

This policy describes our practices in plain language. It is provided for transparency and does not constitute legal advice to you or your facility.
