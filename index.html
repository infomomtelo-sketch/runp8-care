How To

Tello

Included in your plan
Tello is an AI assistant and can be wrong. She answers general questions from her own knowledge and compliance questions from what is recorded in Title22 — check anything you would show an inspector. She will not give medical advice about a resident; that is for the nurse or physician.

Audit log

Administrator only
Immutable record of every change to residents, medications, MAR entries, staff, and incidents. Entries are written by the database and cannot be edited or deleted.
